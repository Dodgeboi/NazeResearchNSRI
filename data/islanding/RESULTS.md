# Islanding experiment — exploratory results

> **Exploratory.** No protocol was frozen before this run. There are 15 contrasts and none was prespecified, so intervals are descriptive, not tests. The model has no identified hospital control effects; these are properties of the simulator under its declared assumptions. Generated by `scripts/analyze_islanding_experiment.py` — do not edit by hand.

Scenario bank: seed 20260922, 100 scenarios per (profile, entry point) cell, 3 profiles x 5 entry points = 1500 paired scenarios per condition, 19500 trials. Config `configs/multiobjective_portfolio.yaml`.

Differences are treatment minus reference, paired by scenario, with 95% percentile bootstrap intervals (resampling scenarios within design cells). Negative is better. *Worse* is the share of scenarios where the treatment lost more weighted service-hours.

## Island-count rule, with the spread confound removed

Two islands cut the same edges at both thresholds, so the change in the share of the three-island benefit that two islands recover is attributable to the capacity rule k ≥ 1/(1 − θ). The rule predicts the share is low at θ = 0.60 (k_min = 3) and high at θ = 0.45 (k_min = 2).

| θ | k_min | Metric | 2-island gain | 3-island gain | Share recovered by 2 islands |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.60 | 3 | weighted service-hours | 50.4 | 88.3 | 57% [55%, 59%] |
| 0.60 | 3 | P(sustained outage, any service) | 8.9 pp | 18.2 pp | 49% [43%, 56%] |
| 0.45 | 2 | weighted service-hours | 76.4 | 92.2 | 83% [80%, 85%] |
| 0.45 | 2 | P(sustained outage, any service) | 16.5 pp | 21.7 pp | 76% [70%, 82%] |
