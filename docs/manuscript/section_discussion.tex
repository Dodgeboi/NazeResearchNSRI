\subsection{What the evidence does not establish}
This is one retrospectively selected project, not a representative sample
of security papers. The reviewers' observations shaped both the analyses
and the finite assertion cases. Multiple defects within this project are
not independent replications of the method. Independent application to a
second simulator and blind evaluation of assertions on unseen manuscripts
would be stronger evidence of general usefulness.

The resampling preserves scenario pairing and the experiment's balanced
entry categories, but conditions on the model, parameter distribution,
observed bank, and frozen shortlist. It does not capture unmodeled recovery
processes, uncertainty in the clinical-service construct, or the variability
of choosing a different discovery bank. Pareto selection is discontinuous,
especially with tied or nearly equal objectives. Reported percentile ranges
are descriptive conditional diagnostics; no bootstrap coverage guarantee
is established. The adjusted t intervals are approximate mean inference
and may remain sensitive to tail behavior and finite within-stratum samples.

Earlier parameter and structural sensitivity experiments remain in the
artifact. They cannot identify coefficients absent measured correspondences
between model mechanisms and real controls. The new tariff draws likewise
test a chosen range, not a validated uncertainty distribution. A beneficial
simulated mean contrast does not by itself answer whether a hospital should
buy a control or whether the improvement has clinical importance.

\subsection{Validation failures remain visible}
The historical external-validation registry has nine benchmarks. Two are
numerically scored: one fails and one is loosely consistent. Two additional
benchmarks are recorded as direct qualitative failures; three are not scored
and two are structurally unaddressable. Thus the total of three failures
includes one numeric failure and two qualitative failures; it is not three
failed numeric tests out of two. The loose consistency label is not a
calibration success: a broad simulated profile range contains a survey
estimate from a different population.

The single-facility, 72-hour horizon cannot represent regional propagation
or months of institutional recovery. Binary digital-service availability
cannot represent partial clinical capacity or patient outcomes. These are
construct limitations that more Monte Carlo trials cannot repair. The
correct conclusion is limited to the declared computational experiment.

\subsection{Audit scope, responsibility, and ethics}
The workflow verifies more than bytes, but less than the truth of a paper.
An incorrect source schema or an overly weak assertion can pass every check.
For that reason, the registry, historical witnesses, and analysis plan are
part of the artifact, and human review of each translation is still required.
Hash checking preserves evidence about a run; it cannot establish that a
dirty historical tree matched an uncommitted implementation or prove a
claimed chronology retroactively. Clean source capture in this revision is
a prospective workflow repair, not a revision of those historical facts.

This reanalysis uses existing synthetic trials and public, aggregate source
material. It introduces no patient records, participant recruitment,
deployment against live systems, or new offensive experiments. We make no
claim of institutional ethics approval. The principal foreseeable harm is
overinterpretation as clinical or procurement advice, addressed by reporting
failed benchmarks and keeping modeled outcomes distinct from patient harm.
Author contribution statements and independent domain review must be
completed by the researchers, not inferred from a successful automated run.

The transferable practice is concrete: when a paper claims that a result is
stable, identify whether that means stable frequencies, estimates,
uncertainty intervals, or decision-set membership. Declare the population,
pairing and allocation, and aggregation before checking the assertion.
Report the failures that remain outside the model's scope alongside the
checks that pass.
