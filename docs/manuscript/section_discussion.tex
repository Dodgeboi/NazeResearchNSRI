The main implication is about where to spend validation effort. Endpoint
agreement was the most reassuring result, but shared prices removed most
of its guarantees. Maintaining positive upgrade prices softened this
decline without eliminating it. A decision-maker using this model would
need defensible incremental cost and burden ranges before treating
frontier membership as a stable reason to prefer a portfolio.

The model's temporal dynamics require separate work. Short technical
restoration, prolonged clinical disruption, and a time-annotated harm
record are different outcomes. The external sources document relevant
consequences but do not identify the transitions or control coefficients
that generate them here. CIPHER adds an independently authored check of
construct coverage; it is not an independent defense experiment. The
single-facility graph also excludes correlated multi-hospital incidents
and regional spillover. Future applied validation would need local
service dependencies, containment and restoration timelines, and measured
implementation costs.

The mathematical scope is narrower and precise. Endpoint intervals permit
candidate-specific combinations that a common threshold cannot generate.
Price regions are chosen stress assumptions. The bootstrap conditions on
the observed scenario bank, and the selected-candidate structural experiment
does not search every possible mechanism. The simultaneous mean bound
requires independent scenario draws and is deliberately conservative;
failure to certify is not evidence of inefficiency. Its retained free
baselines illustrate why Pareto efficiency alone is not a recommendation.

The work uses synthetic trials and public sources, with no recruited
participants or private patient records. The CIPHER archive is preserved
under its data license. The earlier THREAT analysis uses a normalized
repository preview export, not the original deposited file, and is retained
as descriptive scope evidence. Published engineering functions test
implementation on independently defined problems, while evaluation
remains within this research workflow. Scientific transport and independent
replication therefore remain distinct from the verified artifact.
