The audited simulation reproduced its numeric outputs while supporting
incorrect statements about their scope and uncertainty. Reanalysis separates
large endpoint-frequency changes from unchanged frontiers, preserves paired
and stratified uncertainty, and shows why one observed shortlist does not
establish stable frontier completeness. Explicit assertions detect the
selected historical contradictions while exposing the assumptions they rely
on. This case supports a limited but useful standard for security experiments:
reproduce the numbers, then make the interpretation specific enough to test.
