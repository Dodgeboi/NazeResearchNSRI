The project links a pathway-specific hospital defense model to explicit
evidence classes and decision-stability checks. Large changes in an endpoint
frequency can coexist with an unchanged observed frontier. The interval
certificate strengthens that observation: \CertGuaranteedTotal{} of
\CertObservedTotal{} efficient portfolios are retained across the full stated
endpoint box, while possible additions remain visible. Structural alternatives,
paired uncertainty, and shortlist resampling expose different limitations
that endpoint retention alone cannot resolve. Published engineering checks
demonstrate that the certificate transfers to other finite objective tables,
including cases where broad bounds are inconclusive. This yields a concrete
reporting principle: state which outcome, candidate set, and uncertainty
region a stability claim covers, and provide a witness when the claim fails.
