\subsection{Structural assumptions can change the decision}
The preserved parameter analysis reports a worst low-versus-high-third
rank correlation of \ParameterWorstRank{} across its evaluated coefficients
and objectives. This is descriptive evidence of varying ranking stability,
not uniform robustness across the parameter space. The separate structural
experiment finds \StructuralOnlyGated{} candidates efficient only with
containment-gated restoration and \StructuralOnlyParallel{} only with
parallel restoration, within its \StructuralCandidates{} selected candidates.
Intermediate mean loss rises from \StructuralIntermediateGated{} to
\StructuralIntermediateParallel{} weighted service-hours when restoration
can proceed before containment.

The modeled mechanism explains the direction: restored nodes re-enter the
network susceptible while an adversary may retain reach. Earlier restoration
can therefore enable reinfection. The high-capacity selected frontier retains
its membership under the alternative, although its objective values change
slightly. Neither parameter rankings nor the endpoint certificate covers
this structural change, because it changes more than the bounded outage
coordinate. This distinguishes three separate claims about stability.
