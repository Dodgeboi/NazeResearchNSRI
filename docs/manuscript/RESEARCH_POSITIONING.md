## Novelty claim that the evidence can support

This project supplies a reproducible case in which large endpoint-frequency changes coexist with unchanged estimated decision sets, pairing and fixed allocation alter uncertainty interpretation, and a reassuring zero shortlist-artifact count does not persist under conditional resampling. Typed, explicit assertions capture selected interpretation errors missed by the project's preceding number/regex checker.

The conjunction and documented security case are the contribution. None of the underlying mathematical identities, variance estimators, testing concepts or sensitivity-analysis ideas is presented as a new invention. We have not established priority over every prior paper or evaluated the approach on independent projects.
