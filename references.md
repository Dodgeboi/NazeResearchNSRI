## Threat landscape and incident evidence

1. **[VERIFIED 2026-07-13]** European Union Agency for Cybersecurity
   (ENISA). *ENISA Threat Landscape: Health Sector (January 2021 –
   March 2023).* July 2023.
   https://www.enisa.europa.eu/publications/health-threat-landscape
   — Basis for: ransomware ≈ 54% of analyzed health-sector threats;
   providers (53%) and hospitals (42%) most affected.

2. **[VERIFIED 2026-07-13]** Neprash HT, McGlave CC, Cross DA, et al.
   *Trends in Ransomware Attacks on US Hospitals, Clinics, and Other
   Health Care Delivery Organizations, 2016–2021.* JAMA Health Forum.
   2022;3(12):e224873. doi:10.1001/jamahealthforum.2022.4873.
   https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama-health-forum/fullarticle/2799961
   — Basis for: annual attacks on delivery organizations more than
   doubled 2016–2021.

3. **[VERIFIED 2026-07-13]** UK National Audit Office. *Investigation:
   WannaCry cyber attack and the NHS.* HC 414, 27 October 2017 (report
   published 2017; Public Accounts Committee follow-up 2018).
   https://www.nao.org.uk/reports/investigation-wannacry-cyber-attack-and-the-nhs/
   — Basis for: ~19,000 cancelled appointments/operations; a third of
   NHS trusts affected; preventable via basic security hygiene.

4. **[VERIFIED 2026-07-13]** Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security
   Agency (CISA), FBI, and HHS. *Ransomware Activity Targeting the
   Healthcare and Public Health Sector.* Joint Advisory AA20-302A,
   28 October 2020.
   https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa20-302a
   — Basis for: sector-specific ransomware threat warning; standard
   defensive guidance (segmentation, backups, patching).

## Defensive guidance and standards

5. **[VERIFIED 2026-07-13]** National Institute of Standards and
   Technology, National Cybersecurity Center of Excellence. *Data
   Integrity: Detecting and Responding to Ransomware and Other
   Destructive Events.* NIST SP 1800-26, December 2020.
   https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/data-integrity-detecting-and-responding-ransomware-and-other-destructive-events
   — Basis for: detection/response and backup-integrity practices that
   motivate the detection and backup-strategy controls in the model.

6. **[VERIFIED 2026-07-13]** National Institute of Standards and
   Technology. *Framework for Improving Critical Infrastructure
   Cybersecurity (Cybersecurity Framework) v1.1 / CSF 2.0.*
   https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
   — Basis for: the Identify/Protect/Detect/Respond/Recover framing
   underlying the modeled control categories.

## Global capacity context

7. **[VERIFIED 2026-07-13]** International Telecommunication Union.
   *Global Cybersecurity Index 2024.* ITU, September 2024.
   https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Cybersecurity/Pages/global-cybersecurity-index.aspx
   — Basis for: wide national variation in cybersecurity capacity and a
   persistent "cybercapacity gap" in skills, staffing, and funding —
   the motivation for comparing neutral capacity profiles.

8. **[VERIFIED 2026-07-13]** World Health Organization. *WHO reports
   fivefold increase in cyber attacks, urges vigilance.* WHO news
   release, 23 April 2020.
   https://www.who.int/news/item/23-04-2020-who-reports-fivefold-increase-in-cyber-attacks-urges-vigilance
   — Basis for: rising cyber threat to health institutions during the
   COVID-19 period.
